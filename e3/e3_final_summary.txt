
E3: Probability-Based Uncertainty and Stock Returns
=====================================================

Panel: 137 stocks (46 high + 91 medium disagreement), 80 quarters, 2005-2024
Scoring: 2 agents (sentiment, fundamental), named mode, quarterly
Total LLM calls: ~19,000

KEY RESULTS (Fama-MacBeth, Newey-West SE):
─────────────────────────────────────────
                                    FM t-stat    DC t-stat
H_sent alone                        -1.16        -1.53
D_sent (direction)                  -3.17***     -2.78***
H_sent × D_sent (interaction)       +3.17***     +2.77***
H_sent (conditional on direction)   +2.00**       —

Monthly panel FM: H_sent t = -2.56** (228 months)

REGIME SPLIT (pooled OLS, HAC SE):
──────────────────────────────────
Bearish stocks: H_sent → lower returns (β=-10.16, t=-5.33***)
Bullish stocks: H_sent → higher returns (β=+6.66, t=+4.59***)

INTERPRETATION:
──────────────
Same asymmetric mechanism as Delta paper:
- High uncertainty AMPLIFIES the directional signal
- When agents are bearish but uncertain: bears are overconfident → lower future returns
- When agents are bullish but uncertain: bulls are overconfident → higher future returns
- The asymmetry suggests overconfidence drives the effect

Medium stocks drive the result (H_sent t=2.05** vs high t=0.14)
→ Signal stronger in less-analyst-covered stocks

momentum t=-1.73* (momentum reversal)
volatility t=1.60 (not significant after controls)
